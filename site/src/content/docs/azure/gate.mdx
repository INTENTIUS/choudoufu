An estate owns the resource groups its configuration declares. An operator applies two templates once:

```
az deployment sub create --location <region> \
  --template-file live/azure/estate-grant.json \
  --parameters estate=<estate> resourceGroups='["<rg>"]' principalId=<object-id>
az deployment sub create --location <region> \
  --template-file live/azure/estate-boundary.json \
  --parameters estate=<estate> resourceGroups='["<rg>"]'
```

## What that buys

The grant gives one principal a role on the estate's groups. The Policy denies a write whose `tofu-estate` is missing or names another estate, and a delete of a tagged resource. Handing over moves the role assignments.

## What it cannot reach

Reads, anything outside the estate's groups, and untaggable types under Policy. Policy applies to every caller, the estate's own included. None of this is measured yet ([GATE.md](https://github.com/INTENTIUS/choudoufu/blob/main/live/azure/GATE.md)).
