A resource is yours once its `labels` carry `tofu-estate` and `tofu-address`.

## The bulk path

```
choudoufu live-import -state=stock.tfstate -estate=my-estate -approve
```

Without `-approve` it previews. Approved, it writes the labels through a labels-only plan.

## What binds on its own

Networks and other types with no `labels` map bind by the name and project you declare.

## Leaving

Copy `.terraform/choudoufu-cache.tfstate` to `terraform.tfstate` and delete `estate.chdf.hcl` ([Undo](/choudoufu/docs/use/migrate/#undo)).
