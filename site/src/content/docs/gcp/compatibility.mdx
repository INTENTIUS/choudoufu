## The live backend

The live backend in `estate.chdf.hcl` takes the place of the `backend` or `cloud` block.

## Expansions and identity arguments

Every expansion works, because the address label has a fixed length.

## Resource types

Stock `hashicorp/google` and `google-beta`. Types with `labels` and `effective_labels` carry the marker, the network skeleton binds by name, and the record holds the rest.

## Running it

`-target` and plan files work.
