description: Two tags on every resource, and your IAM decides who changes it.
---

- [Adopt](/choudoufu/aws/adopt/): bring running resources under management.
- [Gate](/choudoufu/aws/gate/): scope roles by the owner tag.
- [Operate](/choudoufu/aws/operate/): rename, remove, scale, apply from CI.
- [Compatibility](/choudoufu/aws/compatibility/): what live-check refuses.
- [Proof](/choudoufu/aws/proof/): what is proven and where.
